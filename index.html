Execution Stack

Vite + TypeScript frontend, Clerk authentication, Paystack payments, 5SIM + Owlet adapters, Resend notifications, Postgres ledger, and Vercel serverless runtime.

Wallet & Payments

Fund wallet via Paystack and reconcile through signed webhooks with idempotent ledger updates.

5SIM Rentals

Browse available products, rent numbers, monitor OTP state, and auto-refund on timeout failures.

SMM Orders

Sync service catalog from Owlet/Outlet and provide clear lifecycle statuses from queued to completed.
